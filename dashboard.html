Tips for Safe Donation

A few reminders every donor should keep in mind.

  • ✔️ Donate only if you are healthy and have eaten recently.
  • ✔️ Wait at least 8 weeks between whole-blood donations.
  • ✔️ Confirm the recipient's blood group compatibility before donating.
  • ✔️ Carry a valid ID and your donor card to the donation site.
  • ✔️ Toggle your status to Unavailable if you feel unwell.
